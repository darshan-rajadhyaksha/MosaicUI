Components

Circular List

A circular orbit component that displays elements around a center and rotates them continuously, ideal for galleries, dashboards, and navigation menus.

Fluid Dock

A modern dock navigation with fluid hover animations and interactive tooltips.

Floating Action Menu

A customizable floating action menu that arranges items along a curved arc with configurable radius and spacing.

Marquee

A flexible scrolling layout for showcasing repeating content like logos, announcements, or testimonials.

Section Navigator

A customizable section picker for navigating between content sections.

Text Effects

Circular Text Animation

A flexible component for rendering text along a circular path with customizable styling and rotation.

Decrypting Text

Displays text with a decrypting animation effect, revealing the final content through randomized characters.

Text Animation

Create dynamic text effects by staggering letters or words with configurable motion and timing, great for hero sections, promotional content, or onboarding screens.

Text Emerge

Animates text by gradually turning blurry letters into clear ones, word or letter by letter.

Typewriter Effect

Animates text like a typewriter, with optional speed, cursor, and styling controls.

Vertical Text Slider

Slides through an list of text vertically, pausing briefly on each item before transitioning to the next.

A dynamic night sky background with twinkling stars, customizable density, and content layered on top.

Star Field

A dynamic star field background with adjustable speed, creating a sense of motion and depth.

Visual Effects

Aurora Border

A dynamic border component with rotation, blur, and glow effects for highlighting content.

Border Beam
